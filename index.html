Features

Everything your team needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Beliko replaces the pile of tools you juggle with one quiet, focused workspace.

Tasks that stay clear

Boards, lists and due dates that everyone understands at a glance, without the clutter.

Notes next to the work

Meeting notes and specs live right beside the tasks they describe, so context is never lost.

Focus mode

Silence notifications, pick three tasks and get into flow. Beliko keeps track of the rest.

Decisions, not threads

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Beliko really free for small teams?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for up to five members, with unlimited tasks and notes.

Can I import from my current tool?

You can import from CSV and spreadsheets in one step, and our guides cover moving over from most popular task tools.

What happens when my trial ends?

You'll move to the Starter plan automatically. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade again at any time.

Where is my data stored?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Business customers can choose an EU or US data region.
